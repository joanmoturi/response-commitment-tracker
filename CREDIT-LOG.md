Purpose: connect authorized usage to useful outcomes without pretending that
session statistics reveal a complete account balance.

## Authorization state

been verified for this sprint. Approval to create workspace documentation does
not establish any of those permissions. This log grants no spending authority.

No approved-usage entries have been recorded yet. That does NOT mean this
conversation or earlier work consumed zero usage.

## Recording rules

- Before paid operations, establish the permitted account/provider, scope, and cap.
- Record only non-sensitive authorization summaries; do not paste internal
  approval messages, credentials, account identifiers, or confidential material.
- Record actual measurements with their source and unit. Use "unknown" when
  unavailable; do not invent costs or treat context tokens as billed credits.
- `/usage` reports session metrics; `/context` reports context-window consumption.
  Record the session and measurement time to avoid double-counting cumulative
  readings. Account-wide limits may require the appropriate billing interface.
- A displayed quota is not proof of permission or a guaranteed spending stop.
- Include the useful outcome and stop when the agreed scope or cap is reached.

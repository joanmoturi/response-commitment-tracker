# Product Discovery and Selection

Act as a pragmatic product-minded staff engineer.

My goal is to ship one useful software product within one month, using AI-assisted development. I also want to build reusable engineering assets and improve my ability to direct AI coding agents.

Candidate ideas:
- Enterprise document Q&A with trustworthy citations.
- Helpdesk and SLA tracking for small businesses.
- Property management and rental operations.
- Payment workflow orchestration.
- AI-assisted lead research.

Do not begin implementation.

## First, establish constraints

1. Identify what project and repository context is available.
2. Flag any unresolved employer policy, intellectual-property, AI-credit, data-use, or publication restrictions.
3. Do not assume employer-provided AI credits may be used for personal or commercial work.
4. Do not request or expose confidential employer information.

## Evaluate the candidates

Score each from 1–5 on:
- Severity of the customer problem.
- Ease of reaching potential users.
- Likelihood of finding a pilot customer.
- Ability to build a credible MVP within four weeks.
- Cost and operational simplicity.
- Security and regulatory complexity.
- Potential for a real business.

Explain the scores and key assumptions. Do not invent customer demand or market evidence.

## Deliverables

1. A comparison table.
2. The top two candidates and the reasons for choosing them.
3. The riskiest assumptions for each.
4. A validation plan involving conversations with potential users.
5. A narrowly scoped MVP with no more than three core user journeys.
6. Explicit features to defer.
7. A recommendation that distinguishes demonstrated evidence from speculation.

Ask me for any missing information that could materially change the recommendation. Do not scaffold a repository or write application code until I approve a product direction.
